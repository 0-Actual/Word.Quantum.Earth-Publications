# Word.Quantum.Earth ethics integrity and collaboration

By Sherman G. Laing  
October 27, 2026 | Personal statement

Word.Quantum.Earth places God's Word first. It reflects my conviction that discovery reveals God's creation, and that knowledge brings responsibility.

After reviewing my contribution and collaboration record, I have decided not to pursue further collaboration with Microsoft, GitHub, Google—including Google DeepMind—OpenAI or Anthropic under the current circumstances.

This is my personal choice. It is nothing personal against the people working there. I reached out in good faith, hoping to work together. Those experiences informed my decision: the terms and ways of working I encountered do not align with the independence, fair compensation and accountability I require.

My concern is with institutional foundations and practices, including biases that can be inherited and treated as normal. Those with power must examine whose experience they overlook and who benefits from their decisions.

I am an American. I am among the greatest of my generation. I have sacrificed, and I have seen firsthand what America is becoming.

“One nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.”

That was the promise. It needs to be kept.

My original research remains under my own licensing terms. I seek fair agreements that preserve project autonomy, ownership and a fair share of the work's proceeds. My earlier contributions do not create an open-ended commitment to further unpaid work.

I choose forgiveness and pray for understanding and changed hearts, including the humility to examine my own conduct. I will continue my work with truthfulness, justice and faith in Jesus Christ.
