| 7. This veterans' hospital history is separate from the Tuskegee syphilis study. |
| 8. Other targeted populations | [National Archives, AAPI immigration records](https://www.archives.gov/research/aapi/immigration), Chinese exclusion and Immigration Act of 1924. | Chinese and other Asian populations also faced explicit restrictions. The selected records establish specific forms of racial exclusion and their legal mechanisms. They do not establish a single continuous conspiracy, the conduct of every present-day institution, or individual guilt inherited through ancestry. Connecting a particular modern organization to an earlier actor requires additional evidence.

## Algorithmic evidence

Ziad Obermeyer, Brian Powers, Christine Vogeli and Sendhil Mullainathan, “Dissecting racial bias in an algorithm used to manage the health of populations,” *Science* 366 (2019), 447–453. DOI: 10.1126/science.aax2342.

Read the [original paper hosted by the Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/public_events/1548288/privacycon-2020-ziad_obermeyer.pdf), especially page 1's abstract and the discussion of cost as a proxy for need.

The researchers examined a healthcare risk algorithm and identified unequal illness burden at the same score. The paper links the disparity to predicting spending rather than illness in a setting with unequal access. This is evidence about the studied system and mechanism. No replication or new experiment is claimed here, and it is not evidence of misconduct by Microsoft, Anthropic or OpenAI.
